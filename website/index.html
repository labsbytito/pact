Your first few minutes with Pact.

  1. 1

    Download. Unzip. Keep the folder.

    When the verified package is ready, download it and extract the ZIP. Inside, you’ll find START-HERE.html and the pact-extension folder.

  2. 2

    Give Pact a home in Chrome.

    Paste chrome://extensions into Chrome’s address bar. Turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and select the pact-extension folder.

  3. 3

    Open a page. Say hello.

    Use Chrome’s Extensions menu to pin Pact, then click its icon. Start with a question about a supported page.

Chrome’s official installation guide ↗

A few things to know.

How do I connect my Fo account?+

In the extension, open Settings → Connections and choose Connect beside Fo. Complete the sign-in flow in Fo. Services you want to use may need to be connected in Fo first. The website does not sign in or connect accounts for you.

Can I use every service shown here?+

The connection map shows examples of services available through Fo, not services already active in your account. Actual tasks depend on Fo’s available tools, your connections, permissions, and service limits.

Is this a live demo?+

The walkthrough on this website is illustrative. It doesn’t send messages, connect accounts, or execute tasks. The extension download appears when its release package has been verified.

Will Pact understand every page or video?+

Page access depends on Chrome permissions and the website. Video captions are not always available. Check the source context in Pact; when information is missing, provide the relevant text or clarify your request.

Is Pact in the Chrome Web Store?+

This early build uses a downloaded, unpacked extension. Chrome requires the Developer mode installation steps above. A ZIP is not installed by moving it into your Applications folder.
